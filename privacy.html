Privacy policy

Polytechnic Study Hub. Last updated 27 November 2026.

Summary

Polytechnic Study Hub keeps your files, notes, practice scores and settings on your own device. The developer does not collect, receive or store your personal data on any server. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Files you add, your practice scores, recent items, your name (used only for the greeting), and your settings are stored in your browser's storage on this device. Clearing the browser's site data or using Delete all saved files in Settings removes them.

The AI study assistant (optional)

The assistant only works if you add your own Anthropic API key in the app. When you ask the assistant a question, the app sends your question and the notes you choose directly from your device to Anthropic's API. Nothing is sent to the assistant unless you ask it to. Anthropic handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Your API key is stored only on this device and is sent only to Anthropic.

Internet use

The app works offline after its first visit. It connects to the internet only for the AI study assistant.

Children

The app is designed for students. It does not ask for an account, an age or any personal details.

Your choices

You can delete saved files and clear recent items in Settings, turn the assistant off, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling or clearing the site data also removes what is stored on the device.
